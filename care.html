Plant Care Tips

Paudon Ki Dekh Bhaal Kaise Karein?

Chhoti si tawajjo se aap ke paude hamesha sehatmand aur hare bhare rahenge.

Sahi Miqdar Mein Paani

Paani dene se pehle mitti ko ungli se check karein. Jab upar ki mitti khushk mehsoos ho tabhi paani dein. Zyada paani se jadein kharab ho sakti hain.

Munasib Dhoop Aur Roshni

Indoor paudon ko tez dhoop ke bajaye roshan khidki ke paas rakhein, jabke phoolon wale paudon ko rozana subah ki 4 se 5 ghante ki dhoop dein.

Khaad Aur Safai

Mahine mein aik baar organic khaad dalein aur peele ya sookhay patton ko kaat dein taake naye patte tezi se ug sakein.

Patton Par Spray

Garmi aur khushk mausam mein indoor paudon ke patton par halkay paani ka spray karein taake nami barqarar rahe aur dhool saaf ho jaye.

Sahi Gamla Aur Drainage

Gamle ke neeche surakh (drainage hole) hona zaroori hai taake faltu paani bahar nikal jaye aur mitti mein badbu ya fungus na lage.

Keeron Se Hifazat

Patton ke neeche check karte rahein. Agar keere nazar aayein toh neem oil aur paani ka halka spray istemal karein.
